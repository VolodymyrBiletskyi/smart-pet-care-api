## 2. What the Service does

The Service is a record-keeping and reminder tool for people looking after
animals. It lets you:

- keep a profile for each pet, with species, breed, date of birth, sex, weight
  and free-text notes;
- record care and health information over time: feeding, weight, activity,
  sleep, journal entries, conditions, medications, vet visits and preventive
  treatments;
- upload photos and documents and attach them to a pet;
- schedule reminders for recurring care and receive push notifications for
  them;
- use AI-assisted features, which read the records above and produce a chat
  reply, a wellness score or a feeding summary.

## 3. The Service is not veterinary advice

Always consult a qualified veterinarian about your animal's health, and do so
immediately in an emergency or when an animal's condition changes suddenly.
Never delay seeking professional care, change a medication or a dose, or

## 8. AI-assisted features

The chat assistant, the wellness score and the feeding summary send the relevant
pet records - and, for chat, your message and recent conversation - to the
project's AI service, which produces the response. Section 3 governs how much
weight any of it deserves.

Two practical consequences:

- Keep sensitive personal information about yourself or other people out of
  pet notes, journal entries and chat messages. Those fields are free text,
  they are sent to the AI service, and a pet record is not the right place for
  them.
- These features depend on a service that can be slow or unavailable. When it
  is, the feature fails and the rest of the App keeps working.
